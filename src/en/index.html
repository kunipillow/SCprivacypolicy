StratChart

Visual market analyzer. See the seasonal tendencies and anomalies of the world's major stock indices, drawn from decades of data.

View on the App Store

For iPhone and iPad with iOS 15.0 or later.

Markets

Major stock indices of the United States, Canada, Germany, France, the United Kingdom, Australia, Japan, Shanghai, Hong Kong, Taiwan and India, and a U.S. volatility index.

Analyses

  • Annual price paths, overlaid year by year
  • The four-year U.S. presidential cycle (election, post-election, midterm and pre-election years)
  • 100 days before and after each U.S. presidential election
  • Distribution of daily changes by day of the week and by date (this year and the past three years)
  • Consecutive up and down days
  • Peaks and troughs
  • First half vs. second half of each year

Tap the "i" button on each chart to see how to read it.

Free and subscription

All analyses are available for free. Free members see the analyses with a one-week delay; subscribers see them with the latest data.
