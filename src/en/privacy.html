Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-30

1. About this policy

We are StratChart.com ("StratChart", "we" or "us"). This privacy policy explains what information we collect when you use the StratChart app and this website, and how we use it. By using StratChart, you agree to this privacy policy. If you do not agree, please stop using StratChart.

2. Information we collect

When you contact us

When you send a message from the Contact Us form in the app, we receive the following:

  • Your email address (only if you enter it, so that we can reply)
  • The type of inquiry and your message
  • An anonymous user ID issued by our subscription service (RevenueCat), and whether you have a subscription
  • Your device model, operating system version and app version

We use this information only to reply to you and to investigate problems. If you email us, we receive your email address and your message.

Collected automatically when you use the app

  • Usage statistics (Google Firebase Analytics): the screens you view, events such as opening the subscription screen, device and app information, an app instance ID, and an approximate location (such as country) inferred from your IP address. We use this to understand how the app is used and to improve it. We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics): when the app stops unexpectedly, information about where it stopped, your device model, operating system version, app version and an installation ID. We use this to fix problems.
  • App integrity checks (Google Firebase App Check, using Apple's App Attest): information that confirms requests come from the genuine StratChart app. We use this to protect our data from abuse.
  • Purchases (RevenueCat): an anonymous user ID and your purchase and subscription history from the App Store. We use this to provide the subscription.

This information is not linked to your name or email address.

Stored only on your device

Your settings (such as the appearance setting) and downloaded chart data are stored on your device and are not sent to us.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.
  • We do not show advertising.
  • We do not sell your information.

4. Payments

Subscriptions are purchased through Apple. Payment information is processed by Apple, and we do not have access to your payment card information. For details, see Apple's privacy policy.

5. Service providers and where data is stored

We use the following service providers to run StratChart. They process information on our behalf according to their own privacy policies. The information is stored on servers in the United States.

  • Google (Firebase and Google Cloud): Privacy Policy · Privacy and Security in Firebase
  • RevenueCat: Privacy Policy
  • Apple: Privacy Policy

6. How long we keep information

We keep messages you send us for as long as we need them to reply and to manage our service. Usage statistics and crash reports are kept according to the retention settings of the services above.

7. Your requests

You can ask us to show or delete the messages you have sent us. Please contact us from the same email address, or tell us enough details to identify your message.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and in the app, and change the date at the top.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please use the Contact Us form in the app or email us at stratchart@gmail.com.
