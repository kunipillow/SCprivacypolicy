About the Data

How the charts are made

The charts and tables in StratChart are computed from the daily closing values of each index, obtained from publicly available sources. They are updated once a day on trading days, after each market closes.

Accuracy of the data

The data may contain errors, omissions or delays. Free members see the analyses with a one-week delay.

Educational purposes

StratChart is designed for educational purposes and does not provide investment advice. Past tendencies do not guarantee future results. Please make your final investment decisions at your own risk.
